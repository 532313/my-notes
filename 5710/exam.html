PRG、PRF、PRP 与区分实验

PRGs, PRFs, PRPs and distinguishing experiments

先写理想对象是随机串、随机函数还是随机置换。说清对手收到什么，计算同一测试条件在两边成立的概率，再取差值。五卷各有这组构件中的至少一种。

Identify the ideal object as a uniform string, random function or random permutation. State what the adversary sees and compare the probability of the same test in both experiments. Every paper includes at least one primitive in this family.

分组模式、IV 与 CBC 差错

Modes, IVs and CBC error propagation

先写加解密的块关系，再分开讨论改明文、传输出错和重复 IV。以 2021 Q4(b) 和 2025 Q2(b) 比较差错传播，以 2022 Q2(b) 比较 CBC 和 CTR 的 IV 复用。

Write the block equations, then distinguish a changed plaintext, a transmission error and repeated IVs. Compare error propagation in 2021 Q4(b) and 2025 Q2(b), and compare CBC and CTR IV reuse in 2022 Q2(b).

RSA 与数字签名

RSA and digital signatures

关键在裸 RSA 的确定性、随机编码和签名的验证接口。题目一换目的就重写密钥归属。计算手算另看本学期 HW1。

Focus on textbook RSA's determinism, randomized encoding and what a signature verification interface checks. Reassign key roles whenever the goal changes. Use current HW1 for modular-arithmetic practice.

ElGamal、DH 与随机性

ElGamal, DH and randomness

写出公开值、秘密指数、掩码来源。解释新鲜随机数为什么重要，再区分 DDH、可塑性和 CCA。五份卷子都考了这一组，其中以 ElGamal 最稳定。

Identify the public values, secret exponents and mask. Explain fresh randomness, then distinguish DDH, malleability and CCA security. All five papers cover this family, most consistently through ElGamal.

哈希性质与构造判断

Hash properties and constructions

每题先圈出固定的是摘要、给定消息，还是两条消息都可选。近三年各卷 Q3 讨论构造。生日概率另看本学期 HW2 Q1。

Identify whether the digest or one message is fixed, or whether both messages can be chosen. The three most recent Q3s examine constructions. For birthday probability, use current HW2 Q1.

MAC 设计与加密组合

MAC design and combinations with encryption

指出标签覆盖的内容、密钥是否保密、验证在何时发生。再判断顺序、长度和加密组合是否被正确绑定。HMAC 与 GCM 的细节可用本学期 HW2 Q2 检验。

State what the tag covers, whether the key is secret and when verification happens. Check that order, length and the encryption composition are handled. Use current HW2 Q2 for HMAC and GCM details.
